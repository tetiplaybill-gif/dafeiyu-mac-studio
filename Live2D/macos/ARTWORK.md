# Room background

`room-depth.png` was generated on 2026-10-30 with the built-in image_gen tool, then copied into this project. It replaces the flat SVG as the default room. No character was generated or changed; the installed Bailong character is composited independently at runtime. Original generated file remains under Codex generated_images. This is a pre-rendered background with browser parallax, not a navigable 3D mesh.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait livestream virtual room background, 9:16 vertical, high resolution. Primary request: Create a beautiful cozy three-dimensional miniature bedroom studio for a cute white dragon anime virtual streamer, WITHOUT any character or UI. Rendered 3D diorama quality with detailed rounded furnishings, tangible wood grain, thick soft textiles, physically plausible warm indirect illumination, ambient occlusion, substantial cast shadows and clear floor perspective. Camera is fixed facing into a room at a mild downward angle, NOT extreme isometric. A tall recessed arched window on the back wall shows a blue twilight sky and softly blurred city lights; a sage green bed and quilt sit to the right in the middle distance, small wooden desk and bookshelves on the left, warm hanging pendant lamp above. Cream plaster walls, honey oak, sage, dusty teal, little plants, charming but uncluttered. Composition: portrait 9:16; top quarter relatively calm wall space to support later overlay; lower HALF is an open, unobstructed honey oak floor and one large cream oval plush rug with ample space for a character to stand, dance and walk left-to-right. Strong depth from foreground floor through middle furniture to background window. Keep furniture to the sides, don't place anything on the foreground rug. Soft atmospheric dusk window lighting mixes with amber practical lights, delicate volumetric light rays. Style: premium animated-film set, soft realistic 3D rendering, rich tactile materials and graceful lighting, NOT flat vector, NOT line art, NOT a UI screenshot. No text, logos, watermarks, people, animals, characters or faces.
